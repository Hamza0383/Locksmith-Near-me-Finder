Why is my car key not turning in the ignition?

Learn the common reasons why your car key may not turn in the ignition and the steps you can take to troubleshoot the issue.

L
Locksmith Near me Finder September 2026 · 3 min read

Your car key may not turn in the ignition due to several reasons, including a worn-out key, a malfunctioning ignition cylinder, or a steering wheel lock. Understanding these causes can help you troubleshoot the issue effectively.

A car key not turning in the ignition is a common problem that many vehicle owners face. This issue can be frustrating, especially when you're in a hurry. In this article, we will explore the reasons behind this problem, how to troubleshoot it, and when to seek help from a professional locksmith.

What Causes a Car Key to Not Turn?

Several factors can prevent your car key from turning in the ignition. The most common causes include:

  • Worn or Damaged Key: Over time, keys can wear down or become bent, making it difficult for them to engage the ignition mechanism.
  • Ignition Cylinder Issues: The ignition cylinder can become worn or damaged, preventing the key from turning smoothly.
  • Steering Wheel Lock: If the steering wheel is locked, it can prevent the ignition key from turning. This often happens if the wheel is turned after the key is removed.
  • Electrical Problems: Issues with the vehicle's electrical system can also affect the ignition process.

Common Symptoms of Ignition Problems

When your car key doesn't turn in the ignition, you may notice other symptoms indicating an underlying issue. These can include:

  • Difficulty Inserting the Key: If the key feels stiff or doesn't fit into the ignition slot, it could be a sign of a problem.
  • Unusual Noises: Grinding or clicking sounds when attempting to turn the key can indicate a mechanical issue.
  • Key Gets Stuck: If the key gets stuck in the ignition, it may suggest a problem with the ignition cylinder.

How to Troubleshoot the Ignition Issue

Before calling a locksmith, you can try a few troubleshooting steps to identify the problem:

  1. Inspect the Key: Check for any visible signs of damage or bending. If the key is worn, consider getting a replacement.
  2. Check the Steering Wheel: Gently wiggle the steering wheel while attempting to turn the key. This can help disengage a locked steering column.
  3. Examine the Ignition Cylinder: Look for debris or foreign objects that may be obstructing the ignition slot.
  4. Test a Spare Key: If you have a spare key, try using it to see if the problem persists.

When to Call a Professional

If your troubleshooting efforts do not resolve the issue, it may be time to call a professional locksmith. Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does it cost to fix an ignition problem? The cost to fix ignition issues can range from $100 to $300 depending on the specific problem and vehicle make.

2. Can a locksmith make a new key if mine is lost? Yes, most locksmiths can create a new key based on the vehicle's VIN number or by using the ignition cylinder.

3. How long does it take to fix ignition issues? Typically, fixing ignition problems can take anywhere from 30 minutes to a few hours, depending on the complexity of the issue.

4. What should I do if my key breaks in the ignition? If your key breaks in the ignition, avoid trying to remove it yourself. Call a professional locksmith who can safely extract it and provide a replacement.

5. Can weather affect my ignition system? Yes, extreme cold or heat can affect the ignition system, causing keys to stick or not turn properly.
