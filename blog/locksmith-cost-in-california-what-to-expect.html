Locksmith cost in California: what to expect

Understanding locksmith costs in California helps you budget for services and know what to expect during an emergency or routine service.

L
Locksmith Near me Finder September 2026 · 3 min read

The average locksmith cost in California typically ranges from $50 to $150 for basic services, with additional fees for emergencies or specialized work. This article provides a comprehensive overview of locksmith costs, factors influencing pricing, and tips for hiring a locksmith effectively.

A locksmith is a professional who installs, repairs, and maintains locks and security systems. Their services are essential when you find yourself locked out, need a lock replaced, or want to enhance your home security.

What is the average locksmith cost in California?

In 2026, the average locksmith cost in California varies based on the type of service required. Basic services like lockouts and rekeying generally fall between $50 and $150. However, emergency services can escalate to $200 or more, especially during weekends or holidays.

For example, if you need an emergency locksmith in California, the cost might be higher due to the urgency of the call. Hourly rates can also vary, typically ranging from $75 to $150, depending on the locksmith's experience and location.

Factors that affect locksmith pricing

Several factors can influence locksmith pricing in California:

  • Type of service: Basic services like key duplication are less expensive than lock installation or emergency services.
  • Time of day: After-hours services often incur additional fees, sometimes 1.5 to 2 times the regular rate.
  • Location: Urban areas like San Francisco may have higher rates compared to rural areas due to demand and cost of living.
  • Lock complexity: High-security locks or smart locks require specialized tools and skills, increasing the overall cost.

Typical locksmith services and their costs

Here's a breakdown of common locksmith services and their typical costs in California:

  • Lockout service: $50 - $150
  • Rekeying a lock: $20 - $50 per lock
  • Lock installation: $100 - $300 depending on the lock type
  • Key duplication: $2 - $10 per key
  • Safe installation: $150 - $500 based on the safe's size and complexity

For instance, if you are considering lock installation and replacement in California, expect to pay between $100 and $300, depending on the lock type and installation complexity.

Common mistakes when hiring a locksmith

Many individuals make mistakes when hiring a locksmith, leading to unexpected costs or poor service. Here are common pitfalls to avoid:

  • Not getting a written estimate: Always request a written estimate before work begins to avoid surprises.
  • Failing to check credentials: Ensure the locksmith is licensed and insured to protect yourself from liability.
  • Choosing based on price alone: The cheapest option may not provide the best quality service.
  • Not asking about additional fees: Clarify if there are extra charges for after-hours services or specialized work.

When to call a professional locksmith

It's important to know when to call a professional locksmith. If you find yourself locked out of your home or vehicle, or if your locks are damaged, contact a locksmith immediately. Attempting to resolve these issues yourself can lead to further damage and increased costs.
